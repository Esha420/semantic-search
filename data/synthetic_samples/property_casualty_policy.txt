# VERISK GLOBAL PROPERTY & CASUALTY COMMERCIAL POLICY
Policy Number: VGP-2026-8849-COMM
Underwriter: Verisk Commercial Specialty Lines
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Expiration Date: December 31, 2026

## SECTION 1: COVERAGE AND SCHEDULE OF PERILS
1.1 Covered Property: This policy covers commercial buildings, business personal property, and operational inventory located at insured premises scheduled under Endorsement A.
1.2 Covered Perils: Direct physical loss or damage caused by:
  (a) Fire and lightning strikes;
  (b) Windstorm, hurricane, and hail storms;
  (c) Explosion, aircraft impact, and vehicular collision;
  (d) Riot, civil commotion, and vandalism;
  (e) Sprinkler leakage and accidental water discharge from plumbing systems.
1.3 Limits of Liability:
  - Building Total Limit: $5,000,000 per occurrence.
  - Business Personal Property: $1,500,000 per occurrence.
  - Business Interruption / Loss of Income: Maximum 12 consecutive months or $750,000.

## SECTION 2: DEDUCTIBLES AND COINSURANCE
2.1 Standard All-Peril Deductible: $10,000 per occurrence, unless a specific peril deductible applies.
2.2 Named Windstorm and Hurricane Deductible: 3% of the total insured value (TIV) of the affected building, subject to a minimum deductible of $25,000.
2.3 Water Damage Deductible: $15,000 per occurrence for water leaks arising from plumbing or fire suppression failures.
2.4 Coinsurance Clause: The policyholder must maintain insurance coverage equal to at least 80% of the actual replacement cost value (RCV) of the covered structure at the time of loss. Failure to maintain coinsurance will result in proportional penalty reduction on any claim payment.

## SECTION 3: EXCLUSIONS
3.1 Flood and Surface Water: Damage caused by rising surface water, storm surge, tidal overflow, sewer back-up, or ground seepage is strictly excluded unless an explicit Flood Endorsement (Form FL-90) is attached.
3.2 Earth Movement and Earthquake: Soil liquefaction, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, and seismic tremors are excluded.
3.3 Wear, Tear, and Gradual Deterioration: Damage resulting from rust, mold, dry rot, latent defects, or continuous moisture penetration over a duration exceeding 14 calendar days is not covered.
3.4 Cyber and Electronic Data: Erasure, corruption, or ransom extortion of digital records is excluded under this property form.

## SECTION 4: DUTIES IN THE EVENT OF LOSS OR DAMAGE
4.1 Notification Period: The insured must give written notice of any potential claim to the insurer within 30 calendar days of the occurrence.
4.2 Protection of Property: The insured must take reasonable and necessary steps to protect the property from further damage, including boarding windows and tarping roofs.
4.3 Proof of Loss: A formal sworn Proof of Loss document must be submitted within 60 calendar days following the insurer's request, accompanied by detailed repair estimates, inventory logs, and accounting receipts.
